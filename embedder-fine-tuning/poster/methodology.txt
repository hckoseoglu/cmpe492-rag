METHODOLOGY

A) Benchmark Creation
1. Parse each document into self-contained statements (propositions).
2. Group consecutive statements into semantically coherent, variable-length chunks.
3. Generate two queries per chunk (formal + informal); a judge verifies each and
   regenerates failures with feedback.
4. Hybrid search (dense + keyword/BM25) retrieves candidate chunks for each query.
5. A judge labels each candidate as positive, hard-negative, or irrelevant.

B) Fine-Tuning
6. Train the embedder on (query, positive, hard-negative) triplets - positives pull
   closer, hard-negatives push away. Split by query to prevent leakage.
